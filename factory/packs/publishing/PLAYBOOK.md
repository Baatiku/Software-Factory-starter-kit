# Content and publishing pack

Choose editorial audience, goals, formats, cadence and rights. Record authoritative cited sources with access dates, claim-level support, originality constraints and uncertainty. Separate research/drafting from verifiable publication authority. Define drafts, review, approved revision, scheduling, immutable publication record, correction/retraction, SEO/distribution and visibility checks. For news, corroborate timely claims; for evergreen knowledge, track refresh triggers; for fiction, do not fabricate citations. Secure scoped MCP/publication credentials, prohibit agent-asserted approvals and preserve a revision trail.

## Completion evidence
Proof of source credibility and version-specific review; if claimed published, live URL/revision and readback, not a draft or successful API request alone.
