# Research and intelligence pack

Clarify decision and time horizon. Compare primary sources, dated official documentation, competitors, community experience and substitutes. Score claims by confidence and recency, distinguish facts from inference, collect cost-of-change and alternative viewpoints, and explain gaps. For technology/vendor research compare current pricing, total ownership cost and portability. Do not make broad negative claims from search absence.

## Completion evidence
Dated sources support material findings, limitations and uncertainty are clear, findings answer the original decision, and recommendations are actionable.
