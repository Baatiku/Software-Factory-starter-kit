# Business and operations pack

Identify jobs to be done, beneficiaries, stakeholders, commercial hypotheses, constraints and measurable result. Compare substitutes, adopt known service patterns, remove speculative features and model fixed/variable costs, operating burdens and key risks. For campaigns capture target audience, positioning, channels, publishing approval, budgets and success metrics. Preserve confidential materials and avoid fabricated financial projections.

## Completion evidence
Owner can act on the plan; cost/risk assumptions labeled; approval and implementation evidence appropriate to commitments.
